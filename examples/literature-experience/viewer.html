05 / Cross-view questions

Explain the link

    Scientific limit. Scores show how these synthetic peak lists behave under one declared calculation. They do not establish identity, novelty, an MSI level, or a learning-style claim.

    Computation limit. Greedy matching can depend on tie handling; this implementation resolves equal products by stable peak index. A threshold change is a local exploration, not a rerun or attested scientific result.
